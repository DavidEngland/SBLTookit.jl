1. Field Campaign Comparative Analysis

Evaluating tower spatial resolution, vertical level density (Nz), and GSPT inversion feasibility.

GSPT transition inversions depend on high vertical tower resolution to construct non-uniform spatial derivative matrices (D1, D2). While multi-level masts like CASES-99 (55m, Nz=7) and GABLS3 (200m, Nz=20) yield full topological stability mappings, sparse arrays like SHEBA (2 levels) trigger a Bypassed Guard due to stencil under-determination.

2. Complex EOF Wave-Jet Separation Pipeline

Decomposing velocity anomaly matrices into standing jet modes and propagating internal gravity waves.

Using a self-contained matrix Fourier Hilbert transform, real velocity fields are converted to complex analytic signals z(t) = x(t) + i H[x(t)]. Complex SVD extracts spatial mode amplitudes and vertical phase profiles. Low-amplitude phase noise near the surface is neutralized using an amplitude-weighted vertical phase gradient metric.

Jet Standing Mode Criterion:

∇θweighted < 0.02 rad/m → Uniform spatial phase, non-propagating shear feature.

Propagating Wave Mode Criterion:

∇θweighted ≥ 0.02 rad/m → Sloping vertical phase, propagating internal gravity wave.

3. Numerical Stability & Singular Value Mitigation

Preventing runaway cooling bifurcations and division-by-zero quotient singularities near Jet cores.

Under strong nocturnal cooling, the gradient Richardson number Ri = (g/θv · &partial;θv/&partial;z) / [(&partial;u/&partial;z)2 + (&partial;v/&partial;z)2] suffers from division-by-zero singularities near the LLJ nose where wind shear vanishes (&partial;u/&partial;z → 0). SBLToolkit.jl applies Tikhonov-Morozov filtering and Zero-Offset Hyperbolic Regularization (Z0HR) to guarantee bounded numerical updates.

🛡️ Richardson Number Profile: Raw vs Z0HR Regularized

Unregularized Ri spikes to infinity at the Jet nose (30m), whereas Z0HR maintains smooth, differentiable stability bounds.

Track A Primitive Regularization

Applies Tikhonov-Morozov filtering directly to velocity and potential temperature profiles under a Modified Morozov Discrepancy Principle (MDP). Matches smoothing scale to noise floor δ.

Zero-Offset Hyperbolic Scheme (Z0HR)

Replaces singular quotient denominators with hyperbolic smoothing operators sqrt(x2 + ε2). Eliminates division-by-zero without altering physical equilibria.

Tangential Cone Condition

Ensures non-linear stability bound τ2 ≥ τ1(3 + 2γ), guaranteeing a unique regularization parameter α for non-linear inverse convergence.

4. Quality Control Gating & GSPT Framework Architecture

Triple-Point Dispersion QC validation and multi-phase prognostic GSPT integration.

To prevent silent numerical corruption from creating false regime transitions, SBLToolkit.jl enforces a strict Quality Control pipeline using Triple-Point Dispersion (δTP) alongside schema adapters (`src/ultra/adapters/`) to sanitize incoming NetCDF tower streams.
