2. Complex EOF Wave-Jet Separation Pipeline

Decomposing velocity anomaly matrices into standing jet modes and propagating internal gravity waves.

4. Numerical Stability & Singular Value Mitigation

Preventing runaway cooling bifurcations and division-by-zero quotient singularities near Jet cores.

Under strong nocturnal cooling, the gradient Richardson number Ri = (g/θv · &partial;θv/&partial;z) / [(&partial;u/&partial;z)2 + (&partial;v/&partial;z)2] suffers from division-by-zero singularities near the LLJ nose where wind shear vanishes (&partial;u/&partial;z → 0). SBLToolkit.jl applies Tikhonov-Morozov filtering and Zero-Offset Hyperbolic Regularization (Z0HR) to guarantee bounded numerical updates.

🛡️ Richardson Number Profile: Raw vs Z0HR Regularized

Unregularized Ri spikes to infinity at the Jet nose (30m), whereas Z0HR maintains smooth, differentiable stability bounds.

Track A Primitive Regularization

Applies Tikhonov-Morozov filtering directly to velocity and potential temperature profiles under a Modified Morozov Discrepancy Principle (MDP). Matches smoothing scale to noise floor δ.

Zero-Offset Hyperbolic Scheme (Z0HR)

Replaces singular quotient denominators with hyperbolic smoothing operators sqrt(x2 + ε2). Eliminates division-by-zero without altering physical equilibria.

Tangential Cone Condition

Ensures non-linear stability bound τ2 ≥ τ1(3 + 2γ), guaranteeing a unique regularization parameter α for non-linear inverse convergence.
