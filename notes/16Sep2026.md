In **Generalized Similarity Profile Theory (GSPT)**, the **height-resolved curvature metric decomposition** provides a differential-geometric framework for partitioning the observed vertical spatial curvature of the gradient Richardson number (\(Ri_g\)) into distinct physical and coordinate-mapping components.

By projecting the continuous vertical profile \(Ri_g(z) = \mathcal{R}(\zeta(z))\) as a composite function of the Monin–Obukhov similarity coordinate \(\zeta(z) \equiv \frac{z}{L(z)}\) (where \(L(z)\) is the height-varying local Obukhov length profile), GSPT reveals whether a sharp structural feature or "knee" in a 1D vertical sounding represents genuine thermodynamic stability or a spatial coordinate projection geometry artifact.

---

### **1. Mathematical Formulation**

Applying the multi-variable chain rule to differentiate the Richardson closure mapping \(Ri_g(z) = \mathcal{R}(\zeta(z))\) twice with respect to physical height \(z\) yields the exact spatial curvature decomposition:

\[\frac{d^2 Ri_g}{dz^2} \equiv Ri_{zz} = \underbrace{\mathcal{R}''(\zeta) \, \zeta_z^2}_{C_{\text{const}}} + \underbrace{\mathcal{R}'(\zeta) \, \zeta_{zz}}_{C_{\text{coord}}} + \mathcal{E}_{\Delta z} \tag{1}\]

where \(\zeta_z \equiv \frac{d\zeta}{dz} = \frac{1 - \zeta L'(z)}{L(z)}\) is the spatial coordinate Jacobian, and \(\zeta_{zz} \equiv \frac{d^2\zeta}{dz^2} = -\frac{\zeta L''(z)}{L(z)} - \frac{2 L'(z)}{L(z)} \zeta_z\) is the coordinate acceleration/curvature.

---

### **2. Component Breakdown**

1. **Intrinsic / Similarity Closure Curvature (\(C_{\text{const}}\) or \(C_\phi\)):** \(\mathcal{R}''(\zeta) \zeta_z^2\)
   * Measures the physical non-linearity inherited directly from the governing similarity closure \(\mathcal{R}(\zeta)\) while holding the similarity frame fixed.
   * For standard rational similarity functions (\(\mathcal{R}(\zeta) = \frac{\zeta}{1 + \beta \zeta}\) with \(\beta \approx 5.0\)), \(\mathcal{R}''(\zeta) = -\frac{2\beta}{(1 + \beta \zeta)^3}\), which is strictly negative across all stable states (\(\zeta > 0\)). This term reflects smooth, monotonic lower-level thermodynamic stabilization.

2. **Coordinate-Stretching / Mapping Curvature (\(C_{\text{coord}}\)):** \(\mathcal{R}'(\zeta) \zeta_{zz}\)
   * Captures vertical profile warping introduced purely by non-constant vertical turbulent flux divergence (\(L'(z) \neq 0, L''(z) \neq 0\)).
   * Near nocturnal Low-Level Jet (LLJ) cores where vertical wind shear vanishes (\(S \equiv \left|\frac{\partial \mathbf{V}}{\partial z}\right| \to 0\)), intense coordinate compression causes \(C_{\text{coord}}\) to spike strongly positive (\(C_{\text{coord}} \gg 0\)), counteracting or dominating intrinsic thermodynamic curvature.

3. **Discretization Audit Residual (\(\mathcal{E}_{\Delta z}\) or \(E_{\text{error}}\)):**
   * Isolates operator truncation and finite-difference discretization errors incurred when evaluating second spatial derivatives on non-uniform vertical model or tower grids (e.g., \(1.5\text{ m}\) to \(55\text{ m}\) CASES-99 tower spacing).
   * Tracking \(\mathcal{E}_{\Delta z}\) separately prevents grid-spacing artifacts from contaminating physical curvature fields.

---

### **3. Key Physical & Geometric Insights**

#### **A. Unmasking the "Fold Illusion" (\(\zeta_z = 0\))**

At a spatial coordinate turning point \(z^_\) where the coordinate Jacobian vanishes (\(\zeta_z(z^_) = 0 \iff L(z^_) = z^_ L'(z^_)\)):
\[C_{\text{const}}(z^_) = \mathcal{R}''(\zeta^_)(0)^2 = 0 \tag{2}\]
\[\left. \frac{d^2 Ri_g}{dz^2} \right|_{z^_} = C_{\text{coord}}(z^_) = R'(\zeta^_) \zeta_{zz}(z^_) = -\frac{\mathcal{R}'(\zeta^_) z^* L''(z^_)}{[L(z^_)]^2} \tag{3}\]

Because \(C_{\text{const}}\) vanishes identically at a fold, **100% of the observed vertical profile curvature at a coordinate turning point is coordinate-driven (\(C_{\text{coord}}\))**. What single-column models or observationalists classify as a sharp "profile knee" near \(Ri_c \approx 0.25\) is a kinematic coordinate projection artifact—a **Fold Illusion**—rather than a physical TKE collapse. Under the **Geometric–Dynamical Decoupling Principle**, a spatial coordinate fold (\(\zeta_z = 0\)) does not imply a fast eigenvalue zero-crossing (\(\lambda_f = 0\)) or dynamical relaminarization.

#### **B. The Inflection Masking Illusion (\(Ri_{zz} \approx 0\))**

During nocturnal cooling (e.g., near \(z \approx 10\text{ m}\)), positive coordinate-stretching curvature (\(C_{\text{coord}} \approx +0.0036\text{ m}^{-2}\)) can almost perfectly balance negative intrinsic similarity curvature (\(C_{\text{const}} \approx -0.0040\text{ m}^{-2}\)).

This drives net observed curvature to near-zero (\(Ri_{zz} \approx 0\)), making the profile appear visually linear ("straight") even though two active, opposing physical processes are occurring. The exact mathematical condition for inflection masking is:
\[(1 + \beta \zeta) \zeta_{zz} - 2\beta \zeta_z^2 = 0 \iff \frac{\zeta_{zz}}{\zeta_z^2} = \frac{2\beta}{1 + \beta \zeta} \tag{4}\]

#### **C. The Linear Obukhov Test (No-Fold Gate)**

If the Obukhov length profile is strictly linear with height (\(L(z) = L_0 + az\) with \(a \ge 0\)), the coordinate Jacobian satisfies:
\[\zeta_z = \frac{L_0}{(L_0 + az)^2} > 0 \quad \forall z \ge 0 \tag{5}\]
Because \(\zeta_z > 0\) everywhere, **linear flux profiles cannot produce finite-height coordinate turning points**. Non-zero vertical curvature in the Obukhov profile (\(L''(z) \neq 0\)) is mathematically mandatory for finite coordinate folds to exist.
