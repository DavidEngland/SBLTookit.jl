$$1 + \beta \zeta^* = 1 + \beta \left(\frac{z^*}{L(z^*)}\right) = \frac{L(z^*) + \beta z^*}{L(z^*)}$$

$$(1 + \beta \zeta^*)^2 = \frac{(L(z^*) + \beta z^*)^2}{L(z^*)^2}$$
